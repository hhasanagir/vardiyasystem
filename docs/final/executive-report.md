## 1. Project Overview

VardiyaOS is an enterprise-grade, 24/7 hospital radiology shift management system built to serve 5,000+ healthcare employees across multiple institutions. The platform automates shift scheduling, real-time callouts, compliance tracking, and operational reporting for radiology departments — replacing manual, error-prone processes with a unified digital operations hub. VardiyaOS is designed for high-frequency, high-stakes healthcare environments where coverage gaps directly impact patient outcomes.

## 7. Risk & Compliance Posture

The risk register tracks 28 identified risks across operational, security, compliance, and vendor domains. Each risk has an assigned owner, mitigation plan, and scheduled review cycle. Key risk themes addressed:

- Data breach prevention through encryption, access controls, and intrusion detection
- Regulatory alignment via automated compliance checks and consent management
- Operational continuity through disaster recovery drills and multi-region failover
- Vendor lock-in mitigation with containerized, cloud-agnostic architecture

Compliance coverage spans HIPAA (administrative, physical, technical safeguards), GDPR (data protection by design, data subject rights, breach notification), KVKK (data controller obligations, consent management), and SGK (Turkish social security reporting).
